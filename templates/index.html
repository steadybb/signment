Frequently Asked Questions

What Is a Tracking Number & Where Can I Find It? ▼
A tracking number is a unique ID assigned to your shipment. You can find it on your shipping confirmation email, receipt, or the shipping label. DHL tracking numbers typically start with "JD" followed by 10 digits.
When will my tracking information appear? ▼
Tracking information typically appears within 24 hours after your shipment is processed and picked up by DHL. For express shipments, updates may appear within 2-4 hours.
Why is my tracking number not working? ▼
Ensure you've entered the number correctly. Common issues include: the shipment hasn't been processed yet, you're using the wrong format, or the number has expired. If it still doesn't work, contact DHL customer support.
What does "In Transit" mean? ▼
"In Transit" means your shipment is on its way to the destination and is actively moving through the DHL network. It has been picked up and is being transported through various hubs and facilities.
Will I receive email updates? ▼
Yes. If you provide your email when tracking, you'll receive automatic updates about your shipment status. You can also opt-in for notifications via the tracking page.
What do the different statuses mean? ▼
Pending: Shipment information received
In Transit: Package is moving through the network
Out for Delivery: Package is with the courier
Delivered: Package has been delivered
Delayed: Delivery is delayed due to customs, weather, or other reasons
Exception: An unexpected event has occurred
